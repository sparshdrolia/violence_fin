# Third-party attribution

`bit/main.js` (Vector Zero) draws on **omenking/pandemic**
(https://github.com/omenking/pandemic) for two things:

- the idea of a curated real-world country roster (ISO3-style codes/names)
  driving the simulation, and
- the symptom-trait taxonomy (fever, coughing, sneezing, sweating, vomiting,
  fatigue, diarrhea, nausea, pulmonary edema, dementia, hypersensitivity,
  ataxia, kidney failure, depression, hemorrhaging, liver failure, heart
  failure, blindness, hypotonia, encephalitis).
